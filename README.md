# ChainGuard AI: Autonomous Agentic Supply-Chain Intelligence & Trade Policy Compliance System

> **An autonomous predictive intelligence system that sources early warning signals across Google News, X (Twitter), and marine AIS sensors, predicts emerging disruptions before they materialize, formulates alternative paths with deep legal trade-off analysis (Jones Act, EAR dual-use, IATA lithium battery restrictions, CBAM tariffs), enforces certified legal mitigations (and declares immutable legal barriers), propagates ripple effects stage-by-stage through the next parts of the supply chain, and learns continuously from real-world execution outcomes.**

## 4. Strict Country Laws, Trade Policies & Transparent Legal Mitigations

The system **NEVER** bypasses or ignores international laws ("I don't care, I will do that - No!"). It rigorously checks every applicable trade statute:

1. **Merchant Marine Act of 1920 (The Jones Act - 46 U.S.C. § 55102 / 19 CFR § 4.80)**:
   - *Scope*: Coastal cabotage requiring goods moved by water between U.S. ports to use U.S.-flagged vessels.
   - *Penalty*: Immediate cargo seizure and civil forfeiture equal to merchandise value.
   - *Actionable Legal Mitigation*: Transfer cargo at marine terminal to bonded intermodal rail (BNSF/UP) or secure an emergency DHS/MARAD Cabotage Waiver.
2. **U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR 15 CFR § 730–774 / ECCN 3A001 & EAR99)**:
   - *Scope*: Controls dual-use microcontrollers, crypto-accelerators, and SiC power modules.
   - *Penalty*: Fines up to \$1,000,000, 20 years imprisonment, placement on BIS Denied Persons List.
   - *Actionable Legal Mitigation*: Electronically submit Form BIS-748P via SNAP-R or route strictly through Wassenaar Arrangement allied nations under License Exception TSR/ENC.
3. **IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR UN 3480 / PI 965 / 49 CFR § 173.185)**:
   - *Scope*: Strict prohibition of standalone lithium-ion battery cells on commercial passenger aircraft; cargo aircraft require State of Charge (SoC) $\le 30\%$.
   - *Penalty*: Aircraft grounding and FAA civil penalties up to \$96,000 per violation day.
   - *Actionable Legal Mitigation*: Charter dedicated Cargo Aircraft Only (CAO) freighters (Atlas Air 747F), enforce pre-flight cell discharge to $\le 30\%$ SoC, and verify UN 38.3 test report and DGD manifest.
4. **EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) & Section 301/232 Tariffs**:
   - *Scope*: Carbon import border adjustments and ad-valorem customs duties.
   - *Actionable Legal Mitigation*: Route via Foreign Trade Zone (FTZ) bonded warehouse to defer duties and file CBP Form 7501 with Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) origin certificates.
5. **Immutable Legal Barrier Protocol**:
   - If an alternative proposes transshipping through an OFAC-embargoed territory or BIS Entity List destination:
   - **`IMMUTABLE LEGAL BARRIER DETECTED`**: The system strictly refuses to violate the law, rejects the route, and safely redirects to allied corridors.

## 7. Enterprise UI & 8 Consolidated Workspaces

ChainGuard AI features a professional enterprise design system adhering to strict operational intelligence ergonomics:
- **Typography**: IBM Plex Sans throughout the interface, with IBM Plex Mono used for technical identifiers, timestamps, and reference codes.
- **Palette**: Warm off-white workspace (`#f7f7f8`), white content surfaces, charcoal typography (`#1a1d20`), muted slate secondary text (`#64748b`), restrained teal accents (`#0f766e`), amber warnings, and red critical-risk indicators.
- **Layout**: Compact persistent left sidebar, minimal top header, information-dense operational surfaces with thin borders and subtle shadows. No purple gradients, glowing animations, glassmorphism, or decorative emojis.

### The 9 Core Workspaces
1. **Operations Center**: Real-time overview of active critical disruptions, prominent Early Warning publications, upcoming component shortages, affected manufacturing facilities, and pending operational interventions, paired with an events feed and autonomous ReAct agent reasoning stream.
2. **Supply Chain Explorer**: Interactive visualization switcher between Google Maps (Roadmap, Satellite, Hybrid, Terrain with corridor zoom controls) and the Cytoscape.js BOM Dependency Network, backed by an operational exposure rationale callout and entity inspector.
3. **Early Warning Control Room**: Dedicated simulation and signal ingestion command center featuring playback controls (Start, Pause, Resume, Step, Reset, 1x-10x Speed), deterministic 5-stage processing timeline, live signal feed, and event verification with 3 distinct meters (Signal Strength, Event Confidence, Supply-Chain Risk).
4. **Predict & Prevent**: Calibrated statistical delay probabilities (`CalibratedClassifierCV`), quantile lead-time prediction horizons ($[P10, P50, P90]$), supporting signal attribution, and on-demand feature sensitivity testing.
5. **Recovery Planning**: Quantitative trade-off evaluation of candidate preventive interventions vs. the status-quo baseline (cost, feasibility, lead time, capacity constraints, ROI) with direct routing to the Decision Gate.
6. **Compliance & Decisions**: Structured Decision Gate enforcing four explicit outcomes (`PROCEED`, `HUMAN APPROVAL`, `GATHER EVIDENCE`, `BLOCK`), statutory legal citations (Jones Act, EAR, IATA DGR, EU CBAM), human signature authorization, and clearly marked demonstration simulated execution.
7. **SAP Integration**: Enterprise S/4HANA Materials Management console with live MD04 Stock/Requirements list, Exception Codes 10 and 50, BAPI_PO_CREATE1 execution, SAP GTT OData v4 event stream, and ORDERS05 IDoc structure viewer.
8. **Incident History & Model Performance**: Historical disruption lifecycle memory store, full signal evolution inspection from early tweet to verified outcome, similar case retrieval, holdout validation metrics vs. naive ERP baselines, and closed-loop retraining logs.
9. **Data Management**: Searchable database viewer for operational tables (shipments, POs, components, suppliers, facilities), CSV/JSON data ingestion pipeline, and confirmed dataset reset.
